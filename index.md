# Tacita

Private AI chat with your own key. Before a message leaves your iPhone, Tacita replaces names, numbers and keys with placeholders and puts them back in the answer on your device.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy/) · [隐私政策](privacy-zh/)

## Support

Questions, problems or privacy requests: **tacita.app@gmail.com**

Please don't send API keys or private conversations by email.
